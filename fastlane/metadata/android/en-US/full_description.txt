NoiseSleep plays white, pink or brown noise, or any blend in between, to help you fall asleep.

<ul>
<li><b>Colour</b>: blend smoothly from white to pink to brown</li>
<li><b>Warmth</b>: soften the hiss, from bright to deep</li>
<li><b>Sleep timer</b>: 15, 30, 60 or 90 minutes, fading out gently over the last minute</li>
</ul>

The noise is generated live, so there are no loops or seams. It keeps playing with the screen off,
pauses for phone calls and stops when headphones are unplugged.

No internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no tracking.
